Stick mode
Angle: left stick = left hand, right stick = right hand. Point the stick and let go; the direction you held sets the throw.
8 equal slices, one per stick direction. Toward the other hand = crossing: down-in 1 · in 3 · up-in 5 · up 7
Outward = same hand: up-out 6 · out 4 · down-out 2
Straight down = 0. Higher throws are more vertical.
Push distance: push a stick up; the further, the higher (1–7). The furthest point of one push counts.
