Sim 2 · You control the timing

Starts on autopilot. Your first throw takes over, and from then on nothing is thrown unless you throw it. A drop resets to autopilot.

Balls

Autopilot juggles all N's (3 → cascade, 4 → fountain). Keys: - / =

Settings

Tempo sets the autopilot's beat and how high each siteswap value goes: a throw of value v flies for (v − dwell) beats. Your own timing is free.

Controls

Every input throws now. The hand needs a ball: a press up to 0.15 s before a catch throws the ball as soon as it's caught. A hand can hold 2 balls; a third one arriving drops.
